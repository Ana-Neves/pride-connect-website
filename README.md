# 🎨 Pride Connect

Welcome to Pride Connect! 🌈  
Our mission is to promote inclusion and diversity in the job market for the LGBTQIA+ community.  
With our platform, you can explore inclusive job opportunities, access information about labor rights, and find mentorship and support programs.

---

## 🌟 Features
Home Page: Presents Pride Connect and welcomes visitors. 🌟

About Us: Learn who we are and what we do. 💬

Inclusive Jobs: Find job opportunities at companies that value diversity. 🧑‍💻

Labor Rights: Understand your rights and get support on employment issues. ⚖️

Mentorship Programs: Explore and sign up for mentorship programs tailored to your needs. 🎓



 ## 📂 Project Structure
Here’s an overview of the main project components:

HeroSection: The welcome section with an image and inspiring message. 🎉

AboutSection: Information about Pride Connect and our mission. 🏳️‍🌈

JoinUsSection: Invitation to join our mission and explore the API. 🤝

MissionSection: Details about our mission and core values. 💪

MentorshipSection: Form to search for mentorship programs. 🌟

OfferingsSection: What we offer and how we can help you. 🛠️

RightsSection: Select your country to access labor rights information. 🌍

## 🔧 Technologies Used
React: To build the user interface

Axios: For API integration and HTTP request management

CSS: To style and beautify the landing page 🎨
