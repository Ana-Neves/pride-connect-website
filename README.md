# 🎨 Pride Connect

Welcome to **Pride Connect**! 🌈  
Our mission is to promote inclusion and diversity in the job market for the LGBTQIA+ community.

With our platform, you can explore inclusive job opportunities, access information about labor rights, and find mentorship and support programs.

---

## 🌟 Features

- **Home Page**  
  Presents Pride Connect and welcomes visitors. 🌟  

- **About Us**  
  Learn who we are and what we do. 💬  

- **Inclusive Jobs**  
  Discover job opportunities at companies that value diversity. 🧑‍💻  

- **Labor Rights**  
  Understand your rights and get support on employment-related issues. ⚖️  

- **Mentorship Programs**  
  Explore and sign up for mentorship programs tailored to your needs. 🎓  

---

## 📂 Project Structure

Here’s an overview of the main components:

- **HeroSection**  
  Welcome section with an image and an inspiring message. 🎉  

- **AboutSection**  
  Information about Pride Connect and our mission. 🏳️‍🌈  

- **JoinUsSection**  
  Invitation to join our mission and explore the API. 🤝  

- **MissionSection**  
  Details about our mission and core values. 💪  

- **MentorshipSection**  
  Form to search for mentorship programs. 🌟  

- **OfferingsSection**  
  Overview of what we offer and how we can help. 🛠️  

- **RightsSection**  
  Select your country to access labor rights information. 🌍
